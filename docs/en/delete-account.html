Delete your account

Last updated: 8 October 2026

In the app (immediate)

The app is currently in Portuguese; the menu names are shown as they appear in the app.

  1. Open Cuddly and go to Perfil (Profile).
  2. Tap Privacidade e dados (Privacy and data).
  3. Tap Eliminar conta e dados (Delete account and data).
  4. If you use email and password, type your password to confirm. If you sign in with Google and your session is old, the app asks you to sign out and back in before deleting.
  5. Tap Eliminar definitivamente (Delete permanently) and confirm.

Deletion happens immediately and can't be undone. If you want to keep a copy, export your data first from the same screen (JSON or CSV).

What gets deleted

  • If you're the only family member: your account and all the family's data (entries, baby profile, pending invites).
  • If there are other caregivers in the family: your account and your member profile. The baby's entries stay with the remaining caregivers, because they're theirs too.
  • On your phone: the local copy of the data is erased at the same moment.

We don't keep our own backups of the database, so no copy of yours remains with us after deletion.

Without access to the app

If you've already uninstalled the app or can't sign in, send an email to lfrm39@gmail.com from your account's email address, with the subject "Delete Cuddly account". That's how we confirm the request is yours. We delete the account and data as described above and reply to confirm, within 30 days at most.
