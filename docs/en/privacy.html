Privacy policy

Last updated: 8 October 2026 · This is a translation of the Portuguese version, which prevails if they differ.

In short: we only store what you need to log to follow the pregnancy and the baby. That data is seen only by you and the caregivers you invite into your family. We don't sell data, we don't show ads and we don't use analytics or tracking tools. You can export or delete everything at any time, from inside the app.

1. Who we are

Cuddly is an open-source app built as an academic project (IEFP, Portugal, UC00507 — Designing dynamic web pages) by Luís, Sara, Eliseu and Beatriz. The code is available at github.com/lmarques39/cuddly-app. For any privacy question: lfrm39@gmail.com.

2. What data we store

DataWhat for
Account: email and password (or, if you sign in with Google, that account's name and email) Signing in. Your password never reaches us: it's handled by Firebase Authentication.
Profile: name, role in the family (mother, father, caregiver) and phone number, if you give one (optional) Showing who made each entry and identifying family members.
Baby: name or nickname (optional), due date or date of birth, sex (optional), weight and height if you fill them in Adapting the app to the stage (pregnancy or postpartum) and working out weeks and age.
Entries: contractions, breastfeeding, bottles, diapers, sleep, pumping, foods introduced and any reactions, doctor's appointments and the notes you write That's what the app is for: logging and looking back at the history.
Invites: the email of the person you invite as a caregiver Letting that person join your family.

Some of these entries (pregnancy, appointments, food reactions) may reveal health information. We treat them with that in mind: they're only accessible to your family and are never used for anything else.

What we don't collect: location, contacts, photos, advertising identifiers or usage statistics.

3. Where the data lives

  • Entries, profiles and family: in the Cloud Firestore database (Google Firebase), on Google servers in London, United Kingdom (region europe-west2). The UK has a European Union adequacy decision for data protection.
  • Account (email and credentials): in Firebase Authentication, a Google service that may process this data outside the European Union, under Google's data processing terms.
  • On your phone: a copy of your entries, so the app works offline, and your notification preferences (these only exist on the device). Reminders are local notifications created by the phone itself: we don't send notifications from servers.

4. Who sees your data

  • You and the caregivers you invite into your family. Each family can only access its own data, and this is enforced by the database's security rules, not just by the app.
  • Google (Firebase), as the provider that hosts and processes the data on our behalf. It doesn't use it for its own purposes.
  • No one else. We don't sell, share or hand over data to third parties, advertisers or analytics companies.

5. Your rights

  • See and take your data: in Profile (Perfil) → Privacy and data (Privacidade e dados) → Export, you download everything as a JSON file, or just the entries as a table (CSV) to open in Excel or Google Sheets.
  • Correct: you can edit or delete any entry, your name and the baby's details in the app itself.
  • Delete: in Profile → Privacy and data → Delete account. Deletion is immediate. Details in Delete your account.
  • Other requests or complaints: write to lfrm39@gmail.com. You can also complain to the Portuguese data protection authority, the CNPD, or the authority in your country.

6. How long we keep data

For as long as your account exists. When you delete it, the data is erased immediately, with no waiting period: if you're the only family member, the whole family is deleted (entries, baby profile, invites); if there are other caregivers, you leave the family and the baby's entries stay with them.

7. Data about the baby

The app is meant for adults (parents and caregivers). Data about the baby is entered by them; they're responsible for it and can correct or delete it at any time.

8. Disclaimer

Cuddly is a logging tool and is not a substitute for professional medical advice. It doesn't diagnose or make clinical recommendations.

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we update the date at the top of the page. The full change history is public in the repository.
